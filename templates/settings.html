🚂 Deployed on Railway?

The form below saves to a local .env file — which works locally but won't persist on Railway after a redeploy.

Instead, set these as environment variables directly in your Railway project: Dashboard → your service → Variables tab.

Also add APP_URL = your Railway public URL (e.g. https://your-app.up.railway.app) so link-tracking works in emails.
And add DATA_DIR = the mount path of a Railway Volume to keep subscribers & history across redeploys (e.g. /data).
